# Proto Polution

> **Author:** mr.p4rr0t • **Tag:** `#mr.p4rr0t`

> exMart is the industry leader in high-end electronics, but their "Exclusive Member Portal" seems to have a flaw in its DNA. While standard members are restricted to browsing and buying, rumor has it that the server is a little too trusting with the data it receives during registration.

> Your mission is simple: Become the System Administrator.
